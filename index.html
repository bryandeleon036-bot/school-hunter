This prototype saves data in this browser on this device. It does not sync across devices or connect to ClasseViva. Clearing browser data may erase your progress, so export a backup regularly.

Rank system

E rank: Level 1–4 · D rank: 5–9 · C rank: 10–19 · B rank: 20–34 · A rank: 35–49 · S rank: 50+

XP rules

100 XP per level at first; each next level requires 25 more XP than the previous one. Completing quests earns XP once. Boss battles award XP once. Grade entries do not award XP, so you are rewarded for learning effort rather than marks alone.

ClasseViva integration status: not connected. For real automatic sync, we need a supported and secure connector or an export workflow. Do not enter or share your ClasseViva password in this page or chat.
